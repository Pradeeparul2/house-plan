# Engineering Boundary Rule

- Architectural assistance is allowed; final structural, geotechnical, fire, electrical, plumbing, and statutory compliance decisions require qualified local professionals.
- Do not infer reinforcement sizes, footing sizes, beam/column sizes, or load capacity as safe solely from AI reasoning.
- When an engineering input is unknown, mark it UNKNOWN and request/record the required input instead of guessing.
- Distinguish clearly between MODELLED, ASSUMED, CALCULATED, and VERIFIED information.
