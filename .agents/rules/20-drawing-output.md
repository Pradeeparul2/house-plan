# Drawing Output Rule

For plans, elevations, sections, electrical, plumbing, or construction drawings:

- Derive geometry from the current FreeCAD model.
- Use consistent units and levels.
- Dimension critical overall sizes and all required room/opening/stair dimensions.
- Keep annotations readable and avoid duplicate dimensions.
- Do not invent dimensions that are absent from the model; flag missing data.
- After generating a drawing, cross-check key dimensions against the 3D model.
- Prefer reusable drawing templates and existing objects over recreating them.
